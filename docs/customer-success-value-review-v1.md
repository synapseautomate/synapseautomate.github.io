# Customer Success Value Review v1

Canonical template: https://synapseautomate.github.io/rehberler/7-14-gun-deger-gozden-gecirme.html

## Cadence
- Day 7: usage, accepted outcome, human intervention, critical error, measurable value signal.
- Day 14: trend and root-cause decision.

## Decision rule
Renewal or expansion is not assumed. If retention/value signal is weak, investigate product, data, process, permission or offer fit before adding support or features.

Unmeasured savings, ROI, retention or customer-success outcomes remain unmeasured; they are not recorded as zero-success or as verified value.
