## 5 kamu yorumu — dil kuralı
Hedef gönderi İngilizceyse EN, Türkçeyse TR kullan. Link bırakma, pitch yapma.

### EN 1 — human-in-the-loop
The part that often gets underestimated is the handoff design. If the system cannot show the source, uncertainty and a clear escalation path, “automation rate” is a weak success metric. Total time to an approved outcome is usually much more useful.

### EN 2 — AI reliability
A strong AI workflow is not just a better prompt. Structured inputs, deterministic checks, provenance and explicit failure behavior matter because they make the system testable when edge cases appear.

### EN 3 — finance / risk
In financial workflows, the interesting question is less “Can AI answer this?” and more “Which part can be automated safely, which rule should stay deterministic, and which decision must remain with an authorized human?”

### EN 4 — evaluation
I like the emphasis on measurement. A benchmark becomes much more useful when it includes critical-error rate, human correction time and escalation accuracy—not only average accuracy.

### EN 5 — AI search / content quality
This also applies to AI-search visibility: publishing more pages is not the same as creating more evidence. A clear methodology, direct answers, sourceable data and consistent entity information are far more durable than scaled low-value content.

### TR kısa alternatif
Burada kritik nokta bence otomasyon oranı değil; onaylı sonuca toplam süre, hata riski ve insan düzeltme yükü. Kaynak ve devralma noktası görünür değilse sistem hızlı olsa da güvenilir olmayabilir.
