# İnsan Devri SOP

States: AUTO_PROCEED, HUMAN_REVIEW, HUMAN_APPROVAL, STOP_ESCALATE.

- AUTO_PROCEED: reversible, low-impact, source/rule checks pass.
- HUMAN_REVIEW: ambiguity, exception or source conflict.
- HUMAN_APPROVAL: financial, contractual or high-impact commitment.
- STOP_ESCALATE: missing authority, unsafe egress/write, unsupported source or prohibited action.

The reviewer sees source reference, extracted/proposed value, uncertainty/exception, proposed action and consequence before approval. Actions are approve / edit / reject / escalate.
