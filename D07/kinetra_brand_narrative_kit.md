## One-sentence vision

**Kinetra Group builds trustworthy technology brands that turn complex digital work and everyday needs into products people can understand, control and rely on.**

Turkish working version: **Kinetra Group, karmaşık dijital işleri ve günlük ihtiyaçları insanların anlayabildiği, kontrol edebildiği ve güvenebildiği teknoloji ürünlerine dönüştüren markalar kurar.**

## Three business lines

### 1. Synapse Automate

B2B AI automation and digital operations. Focus: measurable workflows, source verification, human approval, evaluation and controlled deployment.

### 2. Kinetra Studios

Consumer product studio. Focus: small, focused software products with clear user value, disciplined experimentation and repeatable product quality.

### 3. Kilory

Consumer nutrition/routine product in the Kinetra portfolio. Public language must describe only live, verifiable product capability; roadmap items are labeled as planned.

## Proof vs. plan rule

### Proof

A statement may be presented as proof only if it is backed by one of these:

- a live public product/page
- a reproducible test or benchmark
- a measured first-party metric
- a real customer result with permission
- a public source that directly supports the statement

Synthetic/spec work is explicitly labeled **synthetic**, **spec**, **public demo** or **redacted**.

### Plan

Roadmap, intended product direction, future markets, future integrations, hiring, revenue goals and unreleased features are presented as **planned**, **targeted**, **exploring** or **in development**. They are never written as completed facts.

## Visual language

- Dark navy / near-black base
- Electric cyan as primary signal color
- Green only for verified/pass/safe states
- Amber for review/uncertainty
- Red only for block/critical states
- Large high-contrast typography; no crowded cards
- One visual idea per frame
- Real logo on transparent background; no black logo rectangle
- Logo, divider, page number and text each have independent safe zones
- No decorative element may cross text or metrics
- Every public creative must be rendered and visually inspected before release

## Voice

- Calm, technically literate, decisive
- Explain business consequence before technical mechanism
- Prefer: source, risk, owner, evidence, measurement, human approval
- Avoid: hype, unverifiable superlatives, “100% accurate”, “guaranteed ROI”, fake scarcity, fake customer proof

## Public / private boundary

### Public-safe

- current brand/product descriptions
- live URLs
- public benchmark methodology and limits
- starting prices already approved for publication
- synthetic examples clearly labeled
- high-level vision and portfolio logic

### Investor/private-only category

Do not place the following in public posts, public GitHub files or customer pages unless separately approved for publication:

- non-public financial projections or runway
- valuation expectations
- confidential cap table or ownership details
- private customer pipeline or personally identifiable lead data
- unreleased partner negotiations
- private model/vendor pricing
- security secrets, credentials or internal access details
- unannounced product roadmap dates

This file defines the boundary; it does not store any confidential values.

## Public quality gate

A Kinetra/Synapse asset is not release-ready until it passes:

1. factual/claim check
2. customer-facing language check
3. visual render check
4. mobile/crop check
5. logo safe-zone check
6. link/CTA check
7. privacy/secret check
8. human-gate check for price, payment, legal, medical, financial or binding actions
