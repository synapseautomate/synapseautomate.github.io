# Synapse → Kinetra Studios Cross-Sell Map

Principle: only permissioned, anonymized or aggregated learnings move across brands. No raw customer data is shared by default.

| Synapse B2B signal | Kinetra Studios product hypothesis | Allowed evidence | Not allowed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repeated return/refund triage pain | ReturnOps decision-support template | Aggregated failure categories | Raw orders, names, emails |
| Catalog quality / feed mismatch | Catalog QA micro-product | Synthetic examples + field taxonomy | Customer catalog without permission |
| Lead follow-up leakage | Vertical follow-up playbooks | Anonymous funnel patterns | Contact lists / CRM exports |
| Approval bottlenecks | Approval-card / audit-log component | Generic state transitions | Internal legal/commercial approvals |
| Agentic-commerce readiness questions | Agent-ready commerce checklist | Public standards + synthetic tests | Proprietary client architecture |

Commercial rule: a Studios hypothesis is created only after the Synapse problem repeats across independent contexts or produces qualified inbound; otherwise it remains a hypothesis, not a product commitment.
