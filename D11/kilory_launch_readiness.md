# Kilory — launch readiness checklist / store asset specification

## Positioning safety
- Nutrition/activity features are framed as informational tracking and user-editable records, not diagnosis or treatment.
- User can edit/correct generated summaries before relying on them.
- No claim of medical accuracy, guaranteed health outcome or clinician replacement.
- Store screenshots must show product behavior, not implied health outcomes.

## Store asset spec
1. App icon: clear at small size, no medical certification symbols.
2. Hero screenshot: user-owned daily view; editable fields visible.
3. Feature screenshot: source/entry context + edit affordance.
4. Safety screenshot: “informational / not medical advice” boundary in plain language.
5. Privacy copy: data categories, purpose, deletion/export path; claims must match actual implementation.
6. Support: reachable support/privacy contact.

## Launch gate
- Feature copy matches shipping behavior.
- No unsupported nutrition/activity health claim.
- User correction flow demonstrated.
- Privacy/safety copy reviewed against actual product data handling before submission.
