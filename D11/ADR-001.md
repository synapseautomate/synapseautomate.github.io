# ADR-001 — Unknown is a valid output

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
A fluent model can make incomplete business input look complete. For operational automation, fabricated completeness is more dangerous than an explicit missing field.

## Decision
Every structured extraction schema must support `null`/`unknown`, provenance per extracted field, and a deterministic route to human review when a required source is missing or contradictory. Consequential external actions stay disabled until approval.

## Consequences
- Lower apparent automation rate is acceptable.
- Missing source evidence is visible instead of silently guessed.
- Human review becomes a designed state, not an exception.
- Tests include missing, duplicate, corrupt and contradictory inputs.
