## 1) Rashad George - risk/impact matrix and human escalation
Target: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rashad-george_aigovernance-responsibleai-aiethics-activity-7493368121009823744-3nfr

**Comment:**
A useful extension to the risk/impact matrix is an explicit stop condition. Approval thresholds tell us when a human must decide; stop conditions tell the system when it is not allowed to decide at all. If the source is unverified, the input conflicts, or authority is unclear, “no action yet” can be the correct production outcome.

## 2) Ahmed Hamza - bounded autonomy and governance
Target: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ahmed-hamza-19760301_aigovernance-agenticai-aiagents-activity-7497764798718410752-0_Jq

**Comment:**
“Bounded autonomy” becomes much more concrete when every action has four visible fields: allowed authority, required evidence, escalation condition, and accountable owner. That turns governance from a policy statement into runtime behavior. The ability to stop safely is as important as the ability to act.

## 3) Hemant Soni - workflow automation and human approval
Target: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/01hemantsoni_future-of-ai-activity-7493361178069340160-nN7p

**Comment:**
Starting with the trigger instead of the model is a strong filter. We would add one more question before automating the workflow: what should explicitly remain manual? Pricing exceptions, irreversible actions, missing-source cases, and high-impact decisions often need a different path than the routine 80%.

## 4) Amit Govil - human attention and exception-based oversight
Target: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/amitgovil_ai-artificialintelligence-automation-activity-7469868707796750336-kFZa

**Comment:**
This is why “human in the loop” should be designed around attention, not just presence. Exception-based review is stronger when the system also measures the gate: edit rate, reject rate, escalation rate, and time-to-review. If a high-risk queue is approved 100% of the time without edits, the control may be ceremonial.

## 5) Srinivas Bommena - runtime oversight and accountability
Target: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sbommena_agentic-ai-changes-the-governance-problem-activity-7471221820206174208-efQZ

**Comment:**
Runtime governance is the key shift. For action-taking systems, the audit trail should capture not only what happened but what evidence existed before the action, which permission allowed it, and why the system did not escalate. That makes “when should the agent stop?” a testable requirement rather than a policy sentence.
