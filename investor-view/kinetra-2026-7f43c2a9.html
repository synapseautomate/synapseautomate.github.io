NOVEMBER 2026 · INVESTOR PRESENTATION14
K/ KINETRA GROUPMOCHI · PRODUCT LOGIC
15 / 29

From personal context to five meaningful daily findings.

Mochi is not chat-first; its main interface is a personalized discovery and decision feed.

01LEARN — Goals, preferences, budget, location, feedback
02SCAN — Web, news, deals, events, products and travel signals
03FILTER — Personal relevance ranking and source verification
04DELIVER — Daily 5: up to five high-value findings
05ACT — Comparisons, drafts and approved supported actions

Key distinction: content is excluded unless it answers: “Why does this matter to this user, and why now?”
