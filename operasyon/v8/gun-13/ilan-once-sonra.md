## SONRA — katalog için İNGİLİZCE PASİF TASLAK
**Title:** I will review order and inventory CSV discrepancies with a scoped findings report

**Overview:** For a small ecommerce operations team with one orders CSV and one stock CSV, this narrow review identifies missing SKUs, duplicate order-ID/SKU pairs, unmatched SKU + warehouse keys, and negative quantities. Stock sufficiency is evaluated only when stock timestamp, warehouse context, reservations and order period are clearly defined. Otherwise it is marked NOT EVALUATED.

**Planned delivery:** Row-and-rule findings CSV, one-page assumptions/exception summary, and reproducibility/version notes. Final fixed scope, pricing, delivery days, file size limits, revision allowance and support terms are **not yet determined**; this text must not be submitted before confirmation.

**Excluded:** live inventory updates, store/ERP integrations, ongoing monitoring, financial outcome guarantees, unlimited scope changes.

**Privacy / samples:** Send no personal order/customer data before a written permission and minimization plan. Samples are labeled “SENTETİK ÖRNEK / GERÇEK MÜŞTERİ VERİSİ DEĞİL”; they are not customer work.

**CTA:** Ask a scope question and submit requirements **inside Upwork** only. No external payment or contact invitation.
